🧠 P vs NP: Complexity Classes

Understanding the most famous unsolved problem in computer science

Drawn assuming P ≠ NP (widely believed, not proven): P and NP-Complete both lie inside NP and do not overlap.
If P = NP, P and NP would be the same class.

The Million Dollar Question: Does P = NP?

If P = NP, every problem we can quickly verify could also be quickly solved.
Most experts believe P ≠ NP, but no one has proven it.

P (Polynomial Time) Problems solvable in O(nk) time, for some constant k.
"Efficiently solvable."
NP (Nondeterministic Polynomial) Solutions can be verified in polynomial time.
Not all known to be solvable quickly (P ⊆ NP).
NP-Complete Hardest problems in NP: every NP problem reduces to them in polynomial time.
If any NPC problem is in P, then P = NP.
