Course sites for the courses I teach at Oakland University. They hold public material that anyone can use: course overviews, lecture slides and interactive visualizations.

Enrolled students: the syllabus, schedule, assignments, quizzes, grades and announcements are on Moodle, and Moodle is authoritative wherever the two differ.

| Course | What is here |

| [CSI 3620 - Data Structures and Algorithms](/csi3620/2026-fall/) | Course overview, [lecture slides for all 21 topics](/csi3620/2026-fall/lectures/), visualizations |

The [algorithm visualizations](/visualizations/algorithms/index.html) are step-by-step pages that run in your browser.

For a general path through programming and AI, outside any course, see [learn.tianlema.com](https://learn.tianlema.com/).

## About this site

- Most of the material is a draft, prepared with AI assistance and checked by automated tests and AI review. It has not yet had an independent human review.
- This is the instructor's own site, not an official Oakland University site.
- The pages set no cookies, run no analytics and load nothing from other sites. GitHub, which hosts the site, keeps its own server logs.
